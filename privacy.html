AI Guy Agency ("we", "us", or "our") operates aiguyagency.com (the "Site"). We provide AI automation services for home service businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact information: Name, email address, and phone number you provide via our contact form or during a discovery call.
  • Business information: Trade type, service area, and business details you share with us during consultations.
  • Communication data: Emails, text messages, and call notes from our interactions with you.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule discovery calls.

4. SMS Communications

By providing your phone number and checking the consent box on our contact form, you opt in to receive SMS text messages from AI Guy Agency at the phone number you provided. SMS messages are sent using Twilio, our third-party SMS service provider.

  • Message frequency: 1-4 messages per month.
  • Message content: Follow-ups about your inquiry, scheduling reminders, and service-related updates.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. You will receive a confirmation reply.
  • Help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance.
  • Carrier fees: Standard message and data rates may apply per your wireless carrier.
  • Carriers supported: AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Sprint, US Cellular, and other major U.S. carriers.
  • No sharing: Your phone number is not shared, sold, or rented to third parties for marketing purposes. It is used solely by AI Guy Agency and Twilio to deliver the SMS messages you opted in to.

By consenting, you acknowledge that you are the owner or authorized user of the phone number provided and that you are at least 18 years old.
